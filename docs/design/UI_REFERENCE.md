# The AuraStage: UI reference (from Julius's 14 target screenshots)

These are written descriptions of the 14 reference screenshots Julius supplied on 26 Sep 2026. They show what the finished platform should look like. You can view the images themselves on the Claude artifact page "AuraStage Design Reference" (https://claude.ai/artifact/X7PXBRrqU2nvNbBWvkBcjP). It's private to Julius's account, and a session can read it with the Artifact tool's `read` action.

## Global design system (every workspace)
- **Theme:** near-black background (#0a0a0c) with dark panels (#121216) and thin borders (#232329). Gold accent (#e8b84b, with a darker #a9822f) for the active state, primary buttons, highlighted words in headlines and the progress bar. Teal/green check icons for "complete" and "ready".
- **Typography:** serif display font for hero headlines, with one or two key words in gold (for example "Turn Your Ideas into a **Powerful Screenplay**"). Clean sans-serif everywhere else. Small uppercase gold letter-spaced eyebrow label above each hero (for example "SCRIPTWRITER").
- **Left sidebar (fixed):** "The AuraStage / AI FILM PRODUCTION STUDIO" logo with a gold "A" mark. Nav order: Dashboard, Scriptwriter, Casting & Characters, Dialogue Intelligence, Scene DNA, Storyboard & Shots, Visual Generation, Audio Studio, Editorial & Timeline, Export & Deliver. A divider, then Project Settings (expandable), Team & Collaboration, Assets Library, Help & Support. The active item is a gold-tinted filled pill.
- **Bottom of sidebar:** current-project card (thumbnail, "Shadows of Lagos", "Feature Film | 2025", a gold progress bar with %, and stats: Scenes, Shots, Days Left).
- **Top bar:** project switcher (thumbnail + title + type/year + chevron). Then the **9-stage stepper**: numbered circles 1–9 joined by dashes (Scriptwriter, Casting & Characters, Dialogue Intelligence, Scene DNA, Storyboard & Shots, Visual Generation, Audio Studio, Editorial & Timeline, Export & Deliver). The current stage is gold with an underline. On the right are a **Save** outline button and a gold primary button ("Next →", "Open Scriptwriter →", "Finish Project →", etc.).
- **Hero band:** wide cinematic image (the Lagos skyline at dusk with the two lead characters). It holds the eyebrow, the serif headline, a one-to-two-line description, and a quote card on the right in italic serif with a gold underline.
- **"Inputs from previous stages" strip:** a row of cards (icon, stage name, what flows in), joined by gold arrows. The current stage's card is outlined in gold. This makes the canonical data flow visible, and it appears on every stage page.
- **Body:** typically three columns. On the left, a scene or item list with search, filter chips (All / With X / In Progress / Completed) and status pills. In the centre, the main editor or preview with tabs. On the right, AI tools, suggestions and checks (a list of tool rows with icon, title, subtitle and chevron).
- **Status pills:** "Completed", "In Progress", "Not Started", "Approved", "Ready", "DNA Complete". Checklists use green ticks ("Continuity Check", "Quality Control", "Character Consistency").

## Per-screen notes

1. **Dashboard** ("Welcome back, Julius / Turn Your Story into an Extraordinary Film"). There's a row of 9 stage cards, each with an image, a number badge, a description, a progress bar with % and an arrow button. Below: **My Projects** list with search, a "+ New Project" gold button, and rows showing thumbnail, title, type/year, progress bar and %. **Project Overview** card has the logline, genre tags and counts (Scenes, Shots, Characters, Locations, Props) plus Recent Activity. **Today's Tasks** is a checklist with stage tags and due dates. **Production Progress** is a donut chart with per-stage %. The top right shows a bell and the user avatar ("Julius, Founder").
2. **Home / marketing** (public). The nav is Home, Features, How It Works, Pricing, Showcase, Resources, plus search, Sign In and a gold Get Started button. The hero is "From Imagination to Extraordinary Films" with "Start Creating Free" (gold), "Watch Demo 2 min", and three tick bullets. Below: 8 feature cards and "A complete film production workflow" (a numbered 1–9 stepper). "Create Any Kind of Story" shows genre tiles (Drama, Historical, Sci-Fi, Action, Documentary, Animation, Romance), then social-proof stats and a quote.
3. **Scriptwriter** ("Turn Your Ideas into a Powerful Screenplay"). Seven sub-step tabs: 1 Project Setup, 2 Story Development, 3 Outline & Structure, 4 Generate Script, 5 Edit & Refine, 6 Scene Breakdown, 7 Character Extraction. The **Project Setup** form has these fields: title, film type (Feature Film / Short Film / Series), target runtime slider, primary genre, subgenre, setting, time period, logline, tone, audience, opening style, ending style and advanced options, plus a gold "Generate Story Outline →" button. The centre is a **Script Editor** in screenplay format (monospace) with toolbar, scene pager and "AI Assist". The right side has AI Tools (Improve Scene, Expand Dialogue, Rephrase/Rewrite, Generate New Scene, Continuity Check) and a Scene Navigator (42). At the bottom, **Script Analysis** shows pages, scenes, characters, locations, acts and structure; **AI Feedback** shows ticks and a "View Full Analysis" button.
4. **Casting & Characters** ("Bring Your Characters to Life"). Tabs: Character List, Casting & Actors, Appearance & Visual DNA, Personality & Backstory, Relationships, Voice DNA, Wardrobe & Props, Scenes & Continuity. The **Characters (18)** list has role pills (Main/Supporting) and "+ Add Character". The **Character Profile** shows a portrait, name/role/age/nationality/occupation/status and a short description. Sub-tabs cover personality, background, motivation, fears, strengths and weaknesses, plus a **Character Arc** by act. Casting Options offer AI Generated, Stock/Library or Custom Upload, a portrait grid and "Generate More Options". Also on the screen: a Character Consistency checklist, Character Relationships, Key Props and Key Wardrobe strips.
5. **Dialogue Intelligence** ("Give Your Characters Authentic Voices"). Tabs: Scene Dialogue, Conversation View, Character Voice & Style, Subtext & Emotion, Dialogue Tools, Scene Analysis, Script Consistency, Approval & Lock. The scene list is on the left. The centre has a scene preview player, with Script View / Dialogue Flow / Emotion Timeline / Character Dynamics / Quick Edit. Dialogue lines have avatar, text, an **Emotion** dropdown, an **Intent** dropdown, a timecode and a play button. On the right: Character Voice & Delivery (speaking style, tone, pace/intensity/formality sliders), AI Dialogue Tools, and Scene Insights (an emotion-arc line chart). At the bottom: Scene Overview & Continuity (previous/current/next scene), a Dialogue Quality Check and More Options.
6. **Scene DNA** ("Turn Every Scene into a Production Blueprint"). This is the central synthesis page. The top strip "How this page works" runs Scriptwriter → Casting → Dialogue → **Scene DNA** → Storyboard. Scenes list with "DNA Complete" pills. The centre is a scene preview with INT/EXT, time, duration and a DNA status, plus tabs Scene Overview / Visual DNA / Performance DNA / Technical DNA / Continuity / Notes & References. It shows purpose, story context, key events, mood chips, reference frames, location, time of day, weather and duration. On the right: Characters in Scene (Lead / Supporting / Voice), Dialogue in Scene, and **Scene DNA Tools** (Generate Scene DNA, Enhance Scene, Check Continuity, Compare Versions, **Lock Scene DNA**). At the bottom are Props / Wardrobe / Location & Environment strips.
7. **Storyboard & Shots** ("Plan Every Shot with Cinematic Precision"). Scenes list with status. The centre is a scene preview, with tabs Storyboard / Shot List / Shot Details / Camera & Movement / Framing & Composition / Scene Flow / Notes. A **storyboard grid** of numbered frames shows shot-type badges (WS/MS/CU), duration and a caption. On the right: Shot Planning Tools, a Scene Shot Summary and a Continuity Check. At the bottom, the **Scene Shot Timeline** is a thumbnail strip with durations and a playhead.
8. **Visual Generation** ("Turn Shot Plans into Stunning Visuals"). Shots-in-scene list with generated/generating/pending states. The centre is a shot player with Previous/Next and Version, generated variations, and "Add to Timeline". Shot details cover shot type, camera angle, movement, lens, duration, fps, composition and focus, plus an **auto-compiled prompt** with badges (location reference applied, style matched, continuity checked). **Generation Controls** on the right: Image→Video / Text→Video / Image→Image, visual model (Runway recommended), quality, aspect ratio, duration, style reference, character-consistency ticks and a gold "Generate Shot" button. Generated Variations V1–V4 come with Compare and Regenerate. There's a scene timeline at the bottom.
9. **Audio Studio** ("Professional Sound for Cinematic Storytelling"). A **multitrack DAW**: groups for Video reference, Dialogue, Foley, SFX, Ambience and Music, each track with M/S/R buttons, coloured clip lanes and a timecode transport. The right side has a video reference, clip properties (start/end/duration/gain/pan/time-stretch) and Tools & Generators (AI dialogue, voice cloning, TTS, lip-sync alignment, Foley, SFX, ambience, music assistant, stems separation, audio cleanup), plus a Scene Audio Intelligence checklist. The bottom holds a **mixer console** of channel strips, a master bus EQ, and loudness metering (LUFS, true peak, LRA).
10. **Editorial & Timeline** ("Assemble, Edit and Perfect Your Film"). Media Library tree (Scenes, Shots, Audio, Graphics & Titles, Color Looks, Templates, Exports, Stills, Project Assets). A viewer with 4K, 24 fps and Rec.709 badges. **Color Grading** has lift/gamma/gain wheels and temperature/tint/contrast/saturation. Multi-track timeline V1–V5 and A1–A9. Effects & Adjustments sit on the right. The bottom has scene thumbnails, Edit Tools (ripple, insert, overwrite, slip, slide, split, trim, match cut, marker, range, group, link, unlink, nest, multi-cam, audio sync) and a Quick Export panel.
11. **Export & Deliver** ("From Final Cut to Every Screen"). Final output sources strip. **Export Presets** (filters: All / Cinema / Broadcast / Streaming / Social / Archive / Custom) include Theatrical Master DCP, Broadcast Master, Streaming Master (4K HDR), YouTube, Social Pack, Festival, M&E, Dialogue Only, International and Custom. There's a preview player and export settings (format, resolution, fps, aspect ratio, colour space, bitrate, render mode, audio, subtitles, chapters, watermark, metadata, hardware acceleration). Also: a **Quality Control & Compliance** checklist with "Run Full QC" and "QC Passed", **Delivery Destinations** with Ready status, a **Render Queue** (progress, time remaining, pause/cancel) and **Exported Deliverables** (file, format, size, date, download). The top-right button reads "Finish Project →".
12. **Project Settings** ("Configure Your Production"). Tabs: Overview, Technical Specs, Visual Style & References, Audio & Workflow, Production Details, Assets & Resources, Delivery Targets, AI & Generation, Integrations, Templates & Presets. Panels are numbered 1–9. Panel 1 is the **Story & Creative Summary**, which is read-only with an "Inherited" badge, says it comes from Scriptwriter, and offers "Edit in Scriptwriter →". This is the canonical-authority rule shown in the UI. Panel 2 is Technical Specifications (resolution, fps, aspect ratio, colour spaces, HDR, bit depth, timebase, start timecode, sample rate, audio config), with a note that changes affect all stages and a link to the impact analysis. Panel 3 is Project Status & Progress. Panel 4 is Visual Style (cinematic style, look & feel, reference images, colour palette, default LUT). Panel 5 is **AI Providers & Generation Settings**: a per-capability provider dropdown (LLM: OpenAI, image: DALL·E, video: Runway, voice: ElevenLabs, music: Suno, lip-sync: HeyGen/D-ID), each with an "Auto Select" toggle, plus generation quality and cost control. Panel 6 is Production Details. Panel 7 is Delivery Targets. Panel 8 is Assets & Resources counts. Panel 9 is Templates & Presets.
13. **Assets Library** ("Your Production Assets. Organised. Searchable. Ready."). Category tabs: Characters, Locations, Props, Costumes & Wardrobe, Vehicles, Environments, Visual References, Audio, Music & Sound, Documents, Graphics & Titles, LUTs & Presets. There's a search box and filters (type, source, scene, character, usage status), a folder tree with counts, and a **grid of asset cards** with a type badge, version (v1–v6), specs and the scenes each is used in. The **detail panel** has Overview / Usage / Metadata / Versions / Comments, file info, used-in scenes, description, tags and related assets. Actions are Download, Replace, Add to Scene and "Open in Visual Generation →".
14. **Help & Support** ("Guidance at Every Step"). A search bar and quick buttons (Getting Started, Video Tutorials, User Guides, FAQ, Contact Support). Popular Guides are video cards. "Browse Help by Module" has 12 module cards. There's a Troubleshooting list and a **System Status** list (platform, AI services, rendering and so on, each marked Operational). **Provider Status** covers OpenAI, Runway, DALL·E, ElevenLabs, Suno, HeyGen, Kling and others. On the right, an **AuraStage Assistant** chat panel is aware of the current page. Support Tickets have status pills, and there's a Resources grid.

## Build notes
- The sample data throughout is the project "Shadows of Lagos": a Feature Film (2025), Thriller/Drama set in Lagos, Nigeria. Characters include Tunde Okafor (protagonist, investigative journalist, 35) and Amara Bello, with 42 scenes and 128 shots. It's useful as seed/demo data.
- Per SRS rule 12, status and health indicators (system status, provider status, readiness %) must come from real evidence and never be decorative. The screenshots show the target look, not permission to fake values.
