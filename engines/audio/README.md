# engines/audio

Implemented:
- `musicSuggestionEngine` v1.0.0 — per-scene music suggestion from the built-in library (the styles `proceduralAudioEngine` plays), with key, tempo, level, placement, instruments and why; says when a scene is better without score.
- `audioSpottingEngine` v1.3.0 (1.3.0, R4: the room tone is named for the scene's acoustic space and returned as `acoustics` — space, why, dialogue and background strips; footsteps and doors timed to each shot's action unless the script already spotted that sound in the shot. 1.2.0: the Score cue follows the music suggestion when given)
- `sceneAcousticsEngine` v1.0.0 (R4 sound realism) — the scene's acoustic space (small room, medium room, large hall, cathedral, outdoors) from the heading (INT/EXT and the place's name, its most specific area first: "ADAMU RESIDENCE - CORRIDOR" is a corridor; bedroom, danfo, study → small; parlour, classroom, courtroom → medium; hall, church, collation centre → large), the Locations & Props description's short "Look:" summary ("cramped", "vast, echoing") — a size word overrides the heading's kind of room only when it describes a room ("a cramped hall"), since a description often covers the whole building — and Scene DNA's atmosphere, with the words it came from; a dialogue strip (clean-up + a reverb send sized to the room, more low cut outdoors) and a background strip (room tone inside, open bed outside); Foley timed to each shot: footsteps where someone walks or runs, a door on the cut where someone comes in (as they leave, near the shot's end) — inside, or wherever a door or gate is named. "Close on…" is a shot size, not a door. — deterministic cue sheet (DX/VO per line, FX from Scene DNA sound candidates, BG ambience, score) with evidence per cue. 1.1.0: each FX/Foley cue is placed where its action line falls in the script, between the spoken lines around it (proportional to the script lines between them); without script positions cues are spread as in 1.0.0.
- `loudnessMeterEngine` v1.0.0 — ITU-R BS.1770-4 K-weighting (libebur128 coefficient derivation at any sample rate), gated integrated loudness, EBU Tech 3342 loudness range, 4× oversampled true peak.

Planned (SRS): `foleyRecommendationEngine`, `adrConformEngine`, `mixRoutingEngine`, `musicCueEngine`.

Each engine has its own folder (see `_template/`) with `index.ts`, `engine.ts`,
`input.schema.ts`, `output.schema.ts`, `rules.ts`, `validator.ts`, `version.ts`, `tests/`.

- `recordedSoundEngine` 1.0.0 (2026-10-02) — real recordings for a cue: `planRecordedSound` reads the cue's words against `categories.ts` (rain, wind, sea, river, traffic, market, crowd, birds, night insects, fire; footsteps, knock, door, gunshot, glass, phone, car, typing, paper, impact, explosion, dog, siren, bell, clock, water, applause, keys) and chooses library recordings by seed — backgrounds spread across the stereo field (up to 4), thunder once in a storm, events in the order the cue names them; `renderRecordedSound` loops backgrounds with equal-power crossfades and peaks at −3 dBFS. Null plan when nothing matches (the caller synthesises). The build's `apps/api/scripts/sfx-categories.json` is generated from `categories.ts` (a test keeps them equal).
- `proceduralAudioEngine` 1.0.0 — AuraStage's built-in sound: deterministic synthesis of ambience (room tone, rain, wind, sea, traffic, birds, night insects, crowd, thunder), effects/Foley (footsteps, knocks, door slam, thunder, gunshot, glass, phone, engine, typing, paper, breath, impact) and score (key, mode and tempo from the mood). Lists every layer it used and why; peaks at −3 dBFS. Placeholder quality by design — never presented as a recording or AI.
- `voiceCastingEngine` 1.0.0 — Voice DNA: a character's voice (gender, age band, register, pace, loudness, English accent, built-in voice variant) from the Casting profile, stable per character, with every reason listed; a line's emotion and intensity adjust the delivery. Provider-neutral: the built-in voice uses the espeak parameters, paid voice providers will use the description. Runs in the browser (Casting → Voice DNA) and on the server (Audio Studio voice generation), so both agree.
- `mixAssistEngine` 1.0.0 — mixing assistants: volume automation that ducks music / ambience under the dialogue actually placed (lines closer than a second merged so the music doesn't pump), and the master gain change that moves the MEASURED integrated loudness onto the target, with the predicted true peak and whether the master limiter catches it.

- `studioMixRenderEngine` v1.0.0 — offline render of an approved Audio Studio mix with the browser's own chain
  (clip fades → fader + automation → high-pass → 3-band EQ → compressor → pan → DX/FX/BG/MX bus → master → limiter,
  post-fader sends to the shared reverb and delay). Follows the Web Audio specification and Chromium's
  DynamicsCompressorKernel, ConvolverNode normalisation and feedback-delay timing. `tests/e2e/audio-parity` renders the
  same mixes with the Audio Studio's own browser code in Chromium: every case matches to 46–137 dB below the signal.
  Used by the render worker, so exported films and stems sound like the approved mixes.
